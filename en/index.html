Whenever you open it, your villagers are living

Village is a village-building game where time keeps flowing even while you're away. Your villagers work, fall in love and grow older while the game is closed. Every time you open it, you'll find your village has grown a little.

6 real hours = 1 village year Seasons change every 90 minutes A village newspaper every morning Music for each season

Only in Village

You can't choose your parents.
In Village, you draw them.

In Japan, people call the luck of the family you're born into oya gacha, "parent gacha". In Village, it's a real draw. After you summon a soul, use the Family Draw to call the family who will raise it. They build a house in your village, move in, and the soul is born as their baby.

The family's house reflects its wealth and the parents' personalities Loving couples have more siblings Kids from strict or quarrelsome homes may rebel A heritage bloodline gives a small nudge to favored jobs and your village's fate

What makes Village special

Every villager has a life story of their own.

Every soul you summon
is born as a baby

Summon souls at the Soul Spring and choose which family they're born into: draw a new family with the Family Draw, or give the baby to a couple already in your village. The rarer the soul, the more likely its talents are to blossom.

Love, marriage,
and the next generation

Each villager lives a whole life: weddings, births, heirs and farewells. Follow your favorite villager and read the record of their life.

Who gets which job?

Farmers, hunters, merchants, craftspeople, teachers, doctors… The better suited your villagers are to their jobs, the richer your village becomes. When a true calling or a rare gift blossoms, your village can change dramatically.

Every village has its own fate

Depending on its jobs and its villagers' talents, your village may become a land of magic, a nation that commands great beasts, an ultra-modern city… How to get there is a secret. Figure it out together with other players.

Four seasons and a morning paper

The seasons turn, and a rainbow follows the rain. If you missed a year, the village newspaper tells you what happened.

Homes, clothes and plazas, your way

Roof and wall colors, festival clothes, plaza decorations and village flags. You can even design your own family crest.

Aim for a great city

From hamlet to village, town, city and finally great city. When your village becomes a great city, a grand landmark rises — different for every fate.

Pricing

Village is free to play. Ranking points can't be bought.

Free to play

All of the village building is free. You earn coins on the days you play and from missions.

Coins

Use coins for soul summons and cosmetic items for homes, clothes and plazas. The first time you buy each coin pack, you get 2.5 times the coins.

Membership (¥500/month)

10 coins every day, an Epic+ ticket, a second village, a deluxe newspaper and more. First-time members get 7 days free.

FAQ and support

Does it cost money to play?

Village is free to play. Coins (for soul summons and cosmetic items) and the membership (¥500/month, with 7 days free for first-time members) are paid.

How do I move to a new phone, or from the web version to the app?

In the game, open "More" → "Transfer (new device)", choose a passphrase and create a transfer code. Enter the code and passphrase on your new device, and your village, coins and membership move with you.

How do I cancel the membership?

If you subscribed in the iPhone app, cancel from iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. If you subscribed on the web, use "View, change or cancel" on the Membership screen in the game. Your perks continue until the end of the paid period.

How old do I need to be?

Village is for players aged 13 and over. If you're under 18, please get your parent or guardian's permission before buying (there's a monthly spending limit).

Which devices are supported?

iPhone (coming soon on the App Store) and web browsers (Safari, Chrome and others). An Android app is in the works.
